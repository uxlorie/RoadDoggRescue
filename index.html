About Us

A loving network rescuing dogs, one foster at a time

We're a 501(c) nonprofit dedicated to saving dogs from being euthanized in overcrowded kill shelters. As a 100% foster-based rescue, every dog in our care lives in a real home — never a kennel — supported by our loving network of volunteers.

  • A registered 501(c) non-profit organization
  • We save dogs from euthanasia at kill shelters
  • 100% foster-based and volunteer-supported
  • We transport community dogs to the low-cost spay/neuter clinic
  • All animals are fully vetted and ready for their forever homes
