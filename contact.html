Surrender Request

Dog Surrender

Surrendering your dog should be a last resort. Please exhaust all other options before resorting to surrendering. You can complete the surrender request form below and if we have an available foster, we may be able to help you — but most of the time, we are completely full and cannot accept surrenders. You cannot surrender a dog that you do not legally own. If you found a stray dog, please contact animal control in your county.
