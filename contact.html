Surrender Request

Need to surrender a dog?

We understand that surrendering a dog is never an easy decision. As a small, foster-based rescue, our ability to take in dogs depends on available foster space. Please submit a surrender request below and we'll respond as soon as we can.

  • Provide as much detail as possible about the dog (age, breed, temperament, medical history).
  • Include clear photos if you can.
  • Let us know your timeline and situation so we can prioritize urgent cases.
